If you've ever watched a TikTok LIVE and seen a Lion or Universe appear on screen — and wondered what it actually cost the viewer and what the creator received — this article gives you the verified 2026 numbers for every major gift.

The math is simple once you know the formula. The confusion comes from TikTok's two-step conversion (coins → Diamonds → cash) and the platform fee that reduces what creators receive from what viewers spend.

⚡ The Formula

Creator payout (Android) = coins × $0.015 × 50%. So a Lion (29,999 coins) = 29,999 × $0.015 × 0.50 = $224.99. On iOS multiply by 35% instead (Apple takes 30% first). All values verified Q1 2026.

How TikTok Gift Values Are Calculated

Viewers purchase TikTok coins at approximately $0.015 per coin (the standard bundle rate for most sizes). When they send a gift, TikTok takes their platform fee — approximately 50% — before the remaining value appears as Diamonds in the creator's wallet. Each Diamond is worth about $0.005, which is the creator's payout rate.

Two factors affect the final creator payout:

  • Platform: If the viewer bought coins on iOS (iPhone/iPad), Apple took 30% first, reducing the effective value of every coin for the creator by about 30%
  • Region: Some gifts — particularly Universe — have different coin prices in different countries. US and UK users see 44,999 coins for Universe; most other countries see 34,999

iOS vs Android — Why Your Payout Differs

The same gift from two different viewers can result in different payouts depending on where they bought their coins. iOS viewers who purchased coins through the App Store trigger Apple's 30% fee, which flows through the system and reduces your effective earnings per coin.

What Expensive Gifts Mean in a Battle

Gift value during a battle is about more than the dollar amount. Timing, Speed round status, and scoreboard position all affect how much impact a large gift has on the outcome.

  • A Lion during a normal period adds 29,999 points to your scoreboard
  • A Lion during a 2x Speed round adds approximately 59,998 points — double the impact
  • A Lion during a 3x Speed round adds approximately 89,997 points — worth nearly three Lions in normal time
  • A Universe sent as a snipe in the final 30 seconds, during Speed, can be worth up to 134,997 points — nearly impossible to overcome

This is why top battle creators are obsessed with Speed round timing. The dollar value of the gift is fixed — but the point value during the right Speed moment can be 3× higher than sending the same gift outside of Speed.
