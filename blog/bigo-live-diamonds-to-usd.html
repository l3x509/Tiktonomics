If you stream on Bigo Live, the Diamonds counter on your screen doesn't tell you what you're actually earning. Diamonds have to pass through a platform cut and convert to Beans before they become withdrawable cash — and the rate most "Bigo Live earnings" articles online quote is the pre-cut number, not what lands in your wallet.

⚡ Key Formula

Creator payout = Diamonds received × 0.5 (platform cut) ÷ 210 (Beans per $1). So 1,000 Diamonds = $2.38. 10,000 Diamonds = $23.81. 100,000 Diamonds = $238.10.

How Bigo Live Diamonds and Creator Payouts Work

Bigo Live runs on three linked currencies, and each conversion step matters:

  • Diamonds — what viewers buy with real money and spend on gifts during a broadcaster's LIVE. Viewer cost ranges from about $0.016 per Diamond (bulk web bundles) to $0.031 per Diamond (smallest in-app bundle).
  • Beans — what the broadcaster actually receives in their wallet after a gift. On standard servers, 1 Diamond received tags 1 Bean, but Bigo's platform cut takes half of that — so broadcasters net 0.5 Beans per Diamond received, not a full Bean.
  • USD — Beans cash out at a fixed 210 Beans = $1 rate through Bigo's verified payment partners.
Full chain, worked example (10,000 Diamonds received)
10,000 × 0.5 ÷ 210 = creator payout
10,000 Diamonds received
→ 5,000 Beans tagged (1:1)
→ 5,000 × 50% platform cut = 2,500 Beans net
→ 2,500 ÷ 210 = $11.90 creator payout

💡 Processing time

Larger withdrawals (over roughly 210,000 Beans, or $1,000) have been reported to take 25-30 business days to process through verified payment partners, versus a faster turnaround for smaller amounts. Budget around this if you're relying on Bigo income for time-sensitive expenses.
