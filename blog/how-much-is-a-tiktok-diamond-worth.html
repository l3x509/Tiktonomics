Quick Answer: The 2026 TikTok Diamond Rate

1 TikTok Diamond = approximately $0.005 USD — or half a US cent. This rate is widely verified across creator communities and third-party calculators in 2026. TikTok doesn't publish an official fixed rate, but $0.005 per diamond is the consistently reported figure used by creator tools and CPAs who work with TikTok income.

The formula: Diamonds × $0.005 = your USD balance. If your Diamond wallet shows 15,000 diamonds, you have approximately $75 available to withdraw.

⚡ Important caveat

TikTok does not publish an official Diamond-to-USD rate. The $0.005 figure is the widely-used working rate verified by creator communities and tools as of 2026. Rates can vary slightly by region and TikTok occasionally adjusts them without announcement. Your Balance screen in-app is always the authoritative source for your actual withdrawable amount.

Coins vs Diamonds — The Difference Explained

TikTok runs a two-sided virtual currency system. Most confusion about diamonds comes from mixing up which currency belongs to which side of the platform.

CurrencyWho uses itHow it's obtainedWhat it does
💰 CoinsViewersPurchased with real money (~$0.015 per coin)Spent on virtual gifts during LIVE streams
💎 DiamondsCreatorsEarned when viewers send giftsWithdrawn as real money via PayPal or bank transfer

Viewers never interact with diamonds. Creators never buy coins. The gifting system converts one into the other — with TikTok taking their platform fee in the middle. This is why the numbers on each side don't match: a 1,000-coin gift doesn't create 1,000 diamonds for the creator.

How Creators Earn Diamonds

1

Viewer buys TikTok Coins

Viewers purchase coins with real money at approximately $0.015 per coin on standard bundles. iOS purchases are slightly more expensive per coin because Apple takes their 30% fee from the viewer's purchase.

2

Viewer sends a gift during your LIVE

Each gift costs a fixed number of coins — from a Rose (1 coin) to a Universe (44,999 coins in the US). The gift appears on screen with an animation visible to everyone in the stream.

3

TikTok converts the gift to Diamonds

TikTok takes their ~50% platform fee and credits the remaining value as Diamonds to your creator wallet. This is the conversion step where the platform's revenue share is applied.

4

You withdraw Diamonds as cash

Once your Diamond balance reaches the minimum threshold (approximately $100 in the US = 20,000 diamonds), you can withdraw via PayPal or bank transfer. Processing takes 3–10 business days depending on your method.

Minimum Withdrawal and How to Cash Out

You can't withdraw diamonds until your balance reaches the minimum threshold. In the US, the commonly reported withdrawal minimum is a $100 balance — meaning 20,000 diamonds. Below that, the withdraw option stays locked.

Key withdrawal facts:

  • Minimum balance: $100 (≈ 20,000 diamonds) in the US
  • Primary method: PayPal — the most widely supported payout option
  • Alternative: Direct bank transfer available in some regions; Payoneer accepted in others
  • Daily cap: TikTok caps withdrawals at $1,000 per day. Larger balances need several days, pulling $1,000 daily until the full amount clears.
  • Processing time: 3–5 business days via PayPal; longer for bank transfers
  • TikTok's fee: None — TikTok does not charge a withdrawal fee. PayPal charges their own standard fee (~2% + fixed amount)
  • Name match required: The payout account name must match your verified identity. Mismatched names are the classic silent failure.
⚠️ Common withdrawal blockers

Under $100 balance — the most common reason the withdraw button is missing.

Unverified PayPal — complete PayPal identity verification before attempting your first withdrawal.

Name mismatch — your TikTok account name must match your PayPal account name exactly. Middle name abbreviations, nicknames, and maiden names all cause failures.

Active Community Guidelines violation — a strike can freeze your monetization including Diamond withdrawal while the violation stands.

Why Your Diamond Payout Might Be Less Than Expected

Several factors can make your actual diamond earnings lower than a simple coin-count calculation would suggest:

  • iOS vs Android purchases: When viewers buy coins on iPhone through the App Store, Apple takes 30% first. This flows through the system and reduces the diamond value of gifts from iOS purchasers by roughly 30%. You have no control over this.
  • Agency deductions: If you're signed with a TikTok LIVE agency, their contract may specify a split of your Diamond earnings before you see them. Agency cuts typically range from 10% to 40% depending on the deal.
  • Gift fraud investigations: TikTok investigates suspected gift fraud including stolen payment cards and circular gifting schemes. Diamonds tied to flagged gifts can be held or clawed back during an investigation.
  • Regional rate differences: The $0.005 per diamond rate can vary slightly by region. Creators outside the US may see marginally different effective rates.
