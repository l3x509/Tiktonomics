How Many Followers Do You Need to Get Paid on TikTok in 2026?

The most common question new TikTok creators ask is also the most misunderstood one. There's no single follower threshold that unlocks "being paid on TikTok" — the platform runs five separate monetization programs, each with its own requirements. The good news: you can start earning real money at just 1,000 followers.

⚡ Quick Answer

LIVE gifts and TikTok Shop unlock at 1,000 followers. The Creator Rewards Program (ad revenue) requires 10,000 followers plus 100,000 views in the last 30 days. TikTok Pulse (premium ad tier) needs 100,000 followers. Brand deals and affiliate links have no follower minimum.

Every program requires: age 18+, account at least 30 days old, no active Community Guidelines violations, two-factor authentication enabled, and personal (not business) account type for LIVE features.

Earning with 0 Followers — UGC and Affiliate

Two income paths require no TikTok followers at all — and both are legitimate earning routes that experienced creators use alongside their follower-based income.

UGC (User Generated Content) means brands pay you to create videos they publish on their own channels. You never post to your account. Brands don't care about your follower count — they care about your ability to produce authentic-looking content that converts. Rates run from $50 to $500 per video for new UGC creators and scale upward with experience and niche.

Affiliate marketing means sharing product links in your bio or videos and earning a commission on sales. TikTok's native Shop affiliate program opens at 1,000 followers, but external affiliate programs (Amazon Associates, ShareASale, Impact) have no TikTok follower requirement. You can earn commissions from a brand's website link in your bio from day one.

1,000 Followers — The First Real Milestone

At 1,000 followers, two significant monetization doors open simultaneously: TikTok LIVE (including gifts and battles) and TikTok Shop affiliate commissions.

LIVE Gifts and Battles

Once you go LIVE, viewers can send virtual gifts purchased with TikTok coins. You receive approximately 50% of the coin value after TikTok's revenue share, converted into Diamonds and then cash. Battle creators — who go live and compete against other creators for audience gifts — often earn more from this single feature than from all other monetization programs combined.

There is no earnings ceiling. A creator with 2,000 followers who battles consistently and has a loyal gifting community can out-earn a creator with 500,000 followers who doesn't go LIVE. The Battle Earnings Calculator on Tiktonomics lets you see exactly what different coin amounts translate to after all fees.

TikTok Shop Affiliate

At 1,000 followers you can apply to become a TikTok Shop affiliate, tag products in your videos, and earn a commission — typically 5 to 25 percent per sale — when viewers buy through your link. Shop commissions are separate from all other TikTok programs and can be substantial for creators in shopping-friendly niches like beauty, home, and fitness.

💡 Battle creators start here

If your goal is battle income, 1,000 followers is all you need to start. Many top-earning battle creators built their entire income stream from this single milestone before ever qualifying for Creator Rewards.

10,000 Followers — Creator Rewards Program

This is the most searched threshold — and the most nuanced. Hitting 10,000 followers is necessary but not sufficient. You also need:

  • 100,000 authentic video views in the last 30 days — this is a rolling window, not a lifetime total. A video that went viral 6 months ago doesn't count unless it's still accumulating views now.
  • Videos at least 60 seconds long — the program only pays on longer-form content
  • Original content only — Duets, Stitches, and reposts don't qualify for revenue
  • Eligible country: US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Mexico, or Brazil

Once enrolled, Creator Rewards pays approximately $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views — up to 25× more than the old Creator Fund. High-CPM niches like finance, tech, and education can see $1.20 or more per 1,000 views.

The 100,000-view requirement is where most creators stall. The way to clear it consistently is posting 5+ videos per week and letting the algorithm find your best content over time. Creators who post every day for 90 days consistently report crossing the threshold — one-off viral videos are less reliable.

100,000 Followers — TikTok Pulse

TikTok Pulse is the premium ad tier that places brand advertising next to the top-performing content. It requires 100,000 followers and being in the top 4% of creators by engagement. Pulse pays a higher CPM than standard Creator Rewards and is automatically applied to eligible videos — there's no separate application.

Brand Deals — No Minimum, But Reality Checks Apply

Brands technically have no minimum follower requirement for deals — they set their own thresholds. In practice, most brands using TikTok's Creator Marketplace start looking at creators around 10,000–50,000 followers. Micro-influencer deals (10K–100K) typically pay $200–$2,000 per video. Mid-tier (100K–500K) pays $2,000–$10,000. Macro (500K+) commands $10,000–$50,000+.

Brands not using TikTok's platform directly often approach creators with smaller audiences — especially in niche categories. A beauty creator with 8,000 highly engaged followers in a specific skincare niche may attract smaller brand deals before reaching the Marketplace threshold.
