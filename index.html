💡 Battle Growth Tips
🎯
Pick active opponents. Battle creators with similar or slightly larger audiences — their viewers will cross over to your stream and gift.
📣
Announce before you go LIVE. Post a video 1–2 hours before your battle so followers can plan to join and gift.
🔥
Set a coin goal on screen. Viewers gift more when they can see a target and a countdown — it creates urgency.
🤝
Co-host strategically. Partner with creators in adjacent niches to tap into their audience.
⏱️
Battle in 10–15 minute rounds. Shorter rounds keep energy high. Multiple battles in one session earn more than one long battle.
📅
Consistency beats virality. Creators who battle 4–5 days/week steadily out-earn those who go viral once. Build the habit.

Yes. All TikTok income is taxable. The IRS considers you self-employed:

— Pay self-employment tax (15.3%) on net earnings
— Pay federal income tax based on your bracket
— Make quarterly estimated payments — TikTok does not withhold taxes

Rule of thumb: set aside 25–35% of every payout.
