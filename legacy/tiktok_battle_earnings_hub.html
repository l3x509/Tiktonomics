💰 Coins & Value
1 TikTok coin is worth approximately $0.0105 USD when purchased, but creators only receive a fraction of that. TikTok sells coins in bundles — the more you buy, the lower the per-coin cost.

As a creator, you receive roughly $0.005 per coin after TikTok's 50% revenue share. So 10,000 coins = ~$50 gross, but your payout is closer to $25.
Step 1 — Viewers buy coins with real money from their app store.

Step 2 — Coins become Diamonds: When viewers send gifts during a LIVE or battle, TikTok converts coins into Diamonds at ~50% — so 1,000 coins gifted = ~500 Diamonds.

Step 3 — Diamonds become cash: You cash out via PayPal or bank transfer. 1 Diamond ≈ $0.005 USD. Minimum withdrawal is $100.

iOS note: If the viewer bought coins through the Apple App Store, Apple takes a 30% cut first — meaning your effective earnings per coin are lower for iOS-sourced gifts.
TikTok keeps approximately 50% of all gift revenue. On iOS, Apple takes an additional 30% from the viewer's payment first.

Both creators earn from their own side's gifts — you keep the coins your supporters send, and your opponent keeps theirs. Winning does not change your payout from gifts already received.

However, winning indirectly increases earnings because it boosts platform visibility, motivates audiences to gift more aggressively, and attracts more viewers to future battles.
In a co-hosted battle, two creators team up on the same side. The coins gifted to your team are split based on a pre-agreed arrangement:

— 50/50 — equal split for equal-sized audiences
— 60/40 — the host who drives more viewers earns more
— 70/30 — when one creator brings significantly more traffic

Always agree on your split before the battle starts. Use the Co-host Split calculator above to see exact take-home amounts after fees.

🧾 Taxes for TikTok Creators
Yes. All TikTok income — gifts, battles, brand deals, Creator Fund, affiliate — is taxable. As a creator the IRS considers you self-employed:

— Pay self-employment tax (15.3%) on net earnings
— Pay federal income tax on top based on your bracket
— Possibly owe state income tax depending on your state
— Make quarterly estimated payments — TikTok does not withhold taxes

Rule of thumb: set aside 25–35% of every payout. Use the Tax Estimator tab to calculate your amount.
Yes, if you earn over $600 in a calendar year. TikTok sends a 1099-NEC by January 31st of the following year.

Even if you earn under $600 and don't receive a 1099, you are still legally required to report that income. The IRS threshold for receiving a form is different from the threshold for reporting — all self-employment income must be reported regardless of amount.

💳 Payouts & Platform
The minimum withdrawal for TikTok LIVE Diamonds is $100 USD, via PayPal (3–5 business days) or bank transfer (5–10 business days).

TikTok may hold new creator earnings for up to 30 days when you first start withdrawing. Make sure your account is fully verified (ID + payment method) to avoid delays.
